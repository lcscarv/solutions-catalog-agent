# Generative AI sales deck: slide outline

1. **Title**: Generative AI that reaches production.
2. **The problem**: most GenAI pilots stall before production because of
   evaluation, cost and trust, not because of the model.
3. **Where it pays off**: document-heavy work, customer support, internal
   knowledge search, content generation with review.
4. **Our approach**: discovery in two weeks, a thin prototype on real data, an
   evaluation set agreed with the business, then production with monitoring.
5. **Architecture pattern**: structured data first for facts and links, retrieval
   for unstructured content, guardrails and human review where errors are costly.
6. **References**: Acme Retail (product descriptions), Volt Energy (field
   technician assistant).
7. **Commercial model**: fixed-price discovery, then time and materials or fixed
   price per phase.
8. **Next step**: a two-hour use case workshop.
